Terms & Privacy Policy

1. Privacy

We respect your privacy. TemporaryMail does not store any personal data permanently. Emails are deleted after a short period and are not linked to your identity.

2. Data Retention

All temporary emails are stored for a short duration (typically 10 minutes) to allow message delivery and are automatically deleted. We do not log IPs or user identifiers.

3. Usage Policy

This service is intended for temporary and anonymous email use only. Any misuse such as spamming, phishing, or illegal activity is strictly prohibited and may result in a ban.

4. Changes to Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. All updates will be reflected on this page. Continued use of the service indicates your acceptance of the current terms.
